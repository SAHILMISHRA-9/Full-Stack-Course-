4.What does it mean for something to be "declaretive" instead of "imperative"
Ans.- Imperative - means we need to give specific step by step intructions on how to accomplish a task.
Declarative - means we can write our code to simply "describe" what should show up
on the page ans allow the rool (react,e.g.) to handle the details on how to put those things on the page.
